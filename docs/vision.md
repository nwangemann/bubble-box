# Vision

Zen simplicity and beauty. Every action is simple, organic and calm.

## The interaction

1. Touch: a bubble begins under the finger, against the glass.
2. Hold: it grows steadily, always slowing, and breathes. It follows the finger with a soft lag.
3. Release: it leaves the glass into the box. A longer hold launches harder; a swipe steers; a larger bubble turns less readily.
4. It meets a wall, squashes, sends a ripple across it and rebounds, eventually back to the viewer.
5. Meeting the glass sends a gentle refracting ripple across the screen (the Glass option).
6. A quick tap on a bubble pops it (the Tap option).

## Principles

- The bubble is the interface. One dot is the only permanent control.
- No scores, text or sound that asks for attention.
- Motion eases. Nothing snaps, flashes or stops dead.
- Responsiveness first: touch response beats visual richness.
- Light on battery: an empty room draws nothing.

## Glossary

- **Box**: the room. Its near face is the screen.
- **Glass**: that near face.
- **Held**: a bubble still under the finger.
- **Charge**: 0 to 1, how far a hold has progressed.
- **Style**: how a bubble looks. **Environment**: how the box looks and bounces. **Feel**: a physics preset.
