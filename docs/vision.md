# Vision

Zen simplicity and beauty. Every action is simple, organic and calm.

## The interaction

1. Touch: a bubble begins under the finger, against the glass.
2. Hold: it grows steadily, always slowing, and breathes. It follows the finger with a soft lag. Every finger grows its own.
3. Release: it leaves the glass into the box. A longer hold launches harder; a swipe steers; a larger bubble turns less readily. A curved swipe spins it, and its path keeps curving.
4. It meets a wall, squashes, sends a ripple across it and rebounds, eventually back to the viewer.
5. Meeting the glass sends a gentle refracting ripple across the screen (the Glass option).
6. Bubbles that meet throw light shaped by the collision (the Meet option).
7. A quick two-finger swipe pops what it crosses (the Pop option chooses how). A tap can also pop (the Tap option).
8. A quick swipe that starts on a bubble flicks it: it is nudged that way, and spins if caught near its edge.
9. Tilting the phone lets bubbles drift gently to the low side, until the hand settles into its new hold.

## Spirit

A solo, experimental project. Artistic fun decides. Lean on intuitive gestures, but broad accessibility is not the priority: be fun and weird, explore widely, then reduce.

## Principles

- The bubble is the interface. One dot is the only permanent control.
- No scores, text or sound that asks for attention.
- Motion eases. Nothing snaps, flashes or stops dead.
- Responsiveness first: touch response beats visual richness.
- Light on battery: an empty room draws nothing.

## Glossary

- **Box**: the room. Its near face is the screen.
- **Glass**: that near face.
- **Held**: a bubble still under the finger.
- **Charge**: 0 to 1, how far a hold has progressed.
- **Burst**: the light from a meeting or a pop. **Style**: how a bubble looks. **Environment**: how the box looks and bounces. **Feel**: a physics preset.
